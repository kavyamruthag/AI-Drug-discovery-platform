Project Summary

This AI Drug Discovery platform predicts potential drugs based on user-input diseases or symptoms using trained machine learning models and embeddings.

Key features:

  • Predicts suitable drugs along with properties: Absorption, Solubility, and Toxicity.
  • Provides analytics for all previously searched diseases, including property trends.
  • Allows downloading of complete datasets for further research and reporting.
  • Designed for educational and research purposes to understand AI-driven drug predictions.
